Build the game with us.

This is a playable discussion kit, not a final design. Use the current four-player rulebook as the common baseline, then try and document alternatives. Every proposed change can be tested and considered; none becomes canon automatically. Participation, feedback and access to the kit are free.

Start with what you have

Gather four people, a printed or hand-drawn cross-board, 64 labeled paper tokens or existing chess pieces, and a pencil. Three players control Mortal factions; one controls Rebirth. Take turns clockwise. No kit purchase, subscription, application or invitation is required.

Separate baseline from experiments. Record the starting rules and each proposed variation before testing. Keep a baseline round for comparison when possible.

Printable 14×14 cross-board

Four 3×3 corners are removed. The remaining 160 squares form the standard four-player chess cross. The center is a shared 2×2 area. This is a blank board: starting-arm assignment and placement presentation remain available for playtest proposals; use the rulebook's four-player setup and record any ambiguity.

For screen-reader users: draw a 14-row by 14-column grid; omit the first and last three columns in the first and last three rows. Mark columns A–N and rows 1–14 on your paper. A tactile board or raised boundaries may be substituted.

Cut-out piece labels · 64 pieces

Each Mortal army: Leader (L), Queen (Q), two Rooks (R), two Bishops (B), two Knights (N), eight Pawns (P). Rebirth army: Leader (L), Rebirth (RB), Death (D), one Rook (R), two Bishops (B), two Knights (N), eight Pawns (P). Cut out the labels or mark household tokens. Letters and faction numbers distinguish pieces without color.

Quick rules reference · baseline only

  1. Set up: each army occupies one arm of the cross. Rebirth takes its army's queen square; Death takes one of its rook squares, chosen by the Rebirth player.
  2. Turn: at the start, clear Veiled from the current player's pieces. Rebirth may spend a Boon Token to disable the aura for a full turn cycle. Move one piece or, on Rebirth's turn, sacrifice an eligible friendly piece for one Boon Token.
  3. Rebirth: moves like a queen. Its Radius of Ruin covers its square and eight neighboring squares. Apply Veiled status at the rulebook's resolution timing; friendly pieces adjacent to Death have Sanctuary.
  4. Veiled: move exactly one square forward, cannot capture, lose special movement; effect ends at the start of the affected owner's next turn. This is not a fixed two-turn timer.
  5. Death: moves like a king, cannot be captured, blocks enemy moves; friendly pieces may pass through but not land on Death.
  6. Soul Reservoir: every three friendly pieces in the Graveyard unlock Rebirth Dash, allowing passage through occupied squares but no landing on one.
  7. Victory: Rebirth wins at 10 Leadership Points from qualifying Withdrawal, Shielding or Coexistence maneuvers (Coexistence max 1 LP per turn). Mortal factions collectively win if Rebirth Veils five of its own friendly pieces cumulatively. Checkmated Leaders are eliminated; their pieces remain neutral obstacles.

Unresolved: Rebirth immunity. The current four-player rulebook states that Rebirth herself is immune to Veiled. Duet's alternative loss-of-control behavior is experimental, not canonical. Test alternatives if desired, but mark them as proposals and do not silently rewrite the baseline.

For complete movement, edge cases and victory definitions, consult the rulebook and continuity policy.
