# Commerce and Rights Gates

The hidden Daddy's Little Mortis Squarespace store is a mock concept-to-product laboratory. It is not rules authority and should remain outside public navigation until each product passes the following gates.

## Product gate
- Creator and ownership recorded.
- Third-party names, characters, likenesses, music, dialogue, logos, and trade dress removed or cleared.
- License compatibility confirmed for code, documentation, art, fonts, and manufacturing files.
- Product marked "concept only — not offered for sale" while uncleared.
- Checkout disabled until approval.
- Price, taxes, fulfillment, refund, shipping, accessibility, privacy, and support terms complete.
- Patreon/Buy Me a Coffee rewards checked against the same product record.

## Variant gate
A themed store item does not promote a variant into canon. A Discord performance, mockup, membership reward, or prototype also does not create commercial permission.

## Review resources
Lex Nova Lawyer may be cited as an entertainment-law educator; her material is educational and not legal advice or project endorsement. For game-specific education and referrals, consult the IGDA Legal SIG, the Video Game Bar Association, and qualified counsel familiar with games, licensing, privacy, online communities, and merchandise.

## Approval record
For every product, record the approving person, date, exact asset version, permitted channels, territory, duration, payment/royalty terms if any, and evidence location. Keep contracts and private correspondence in access-controlled storage, not the public repository.
