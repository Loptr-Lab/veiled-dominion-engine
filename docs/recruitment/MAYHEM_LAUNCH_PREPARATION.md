## Pre-launch evidence checklist and credit

Reference: [Unicool Games, “Crowdfunding strategies: how to run a successful Kickstarter campaign — Part 1,” September 20, 2023](https://unicoolgames.com/crowdfunding-strategies-how-to-run-a-successful-kickstarter-campaign-part-1/). The preparation stages below adapt its team, prototype, playtesting and community-building principles. No text, graphic or template was copied, and no endorsement is implied.

| Stage | Evidence to prepare before claiming readiness |
| --- | --- |
| Team formation | Named responsible owners, bounded scope, rights/credit agreements, voluntary vs paid terms. |
| Proof of concept | Verified four-player flow, documented limitations, reproducible build and stable rules references. |
| Playtesting | Agreed protocol, accessibility accommodations, consent and attributed findings; no invented participation/results. |
| Community | Clear conduct and moderation routes, honest updates and optional approved contact method. |
| Reviews/creator outreach | Permission to contact, accurate prototype disclosure, sponsorship/material-connection disclosures and asset permissions. |
| Social promotion | Owner-reviewed copy/visuals and verified destinations; no automatic campaign. |
| Events/conventions | Feasibility, accessible venue/format, safeguarding, budget and organizer confirmation before dates are announced. |
| Voluntary pre-launch interest | Approved consent, purpose, retention, unsubscribe and system owner before collecting marketing interest; separate from applicant/member records. |
| Campaign finance | Costed production, contingency, taxes/fees, fulfillment/refunds and rights review before real tiers or financial commitments. |
