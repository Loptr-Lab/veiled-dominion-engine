# Gods of Mayhem — Founding 50-unit manufacturing partner brief

**Status:** Negotiation example, not a quotation, contract, signed manufacturer, crowdfunding promise, sales forecast, or approved production order. For partner review only. November 2026.

## Proposed offer
Loptr Lab seeks a **50-copy founding tabletop pilot**, with a manufacturer/production partner that can make an accessible, legible, four-player cross-board game at a viable landed unit cost. A partner may offer reduced upfront production cost or deferred payment **in exchange for a larger, strictly limited share of the first run's collected sales proceeds**, plus an optional earned bonus. No perpetual royalties, ownership in the game IP, exclusivity beyond the pilot, or automatic renewal.

The 50 refers to **50 finished game copies**, not 50 playing pieces. The canonical four-player rulebook requires 64 playing pieces across four armies; this must be reflected in a bill of materials. The print-and-play alpha is separate and remains free.

## Illustrative negotiated scenario — NOT a quote

| Assumption | Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Founding run | 50 copies |
| Hypothetical direct retail price | $79 / copy |
| Gross if all 50 sell | $3,950 |
| Hypothetical manufacturing invoice at $38 / copy | $1,900 |
| Manufacturer's illustrative upfront discount | $600 (reducing upfront cash invoice to $1,300) |
| Hypothetical shipping/fulfilment/transaction fees at $12 per copy | $600 total |
| Collected proceeds after these fees, if all 50 sell | $3,350 |
| Partner success share: 25% of those collected proceeds, capped at $900 | $837.50 |
| Manufacturer total receipts: discounted invoice plus success share | $2,137.50 |
| Loptr Lab remainder after discounted invoice and partner share | $1,212.50 |

**Critical:** The $1,212.50 is **not profit**. It excludes tax, prototype iterations, design/artwork, accessibility testing, returns, replacement stock, advertising, insurance, warehousing, labor, customer support and overhead. If the partner is already being paid the discounted $1,300 invoice, the share must be expressly justified as consideration for the $600 financing/discount and additional deliverables. In this illustrative sold-out case, the partner receives $237.50 more than the original $1,900 invoice; it is not a free discount to Loptr Lab.

**Sales bonus option, not automatically additive:** Instead of the 25% share, a fixed $300 bonus after 40 *paid, non-refunded* copies sell could compensate the partner for taking an upfront $600 discount. That produces $1,600 in manufacturer receipts, $1,750 remaining before other costs at a sold-out 50-copy run, using the same $3,350 post-fee proceeds. The partner would still be $300 below the original invoice. A partner could negotiate a different threshold or larger bonus. **Choose one mechanism** or specify an aggregate hard cap; do not silently combine a share and a bonus.

**Risk / cashflow:** The example assumes the $1,300 discounted invoice is payable upfront. A genuine no-upfront-cash model would require separate negotiated financing, payment security, refund reserves and allocation of unsold inventory risk. Revenue share should be calculated on **actually collected, non-refunded sales less enumerated transaction and fulfilment charges**, not on list price, impressions, preorders that may be refunded, or unrelated game revenue. The agreement must specify whether sales tax and shipping paid by customers are excluded and how discounts, bundles, chargebacks and returns are handled.

## Alternative structures to request
- **Standard purchase order:** manufacturer quotes 1, 10, 25, 50 and 100 copies with no royalty; Loptr Lab pays landed cost, retains sales upside and inventory risk.
- **Discounted founding run:** reduced upfront invoice plus capped share *only on the first 50 serialized copies*, expiring after a fixed term and final reconciliation.
- **Preorder-backed production:** deposits handled with consumer protections and realistic delivery/refund terms; order only after rules, accessibility, rights and proofs are approved.
- **Co-marketing:** documented manufacturer promotion in exchange for attribution and mutually approved content; no implied licensing or permanent brand rights.

It is **not** a comparable small-batch board-game financing quote.

## Non-negotiable boundaries
1. **IP:** Loptr Lab retains its game, trademarks, artwork and canon; production license limited to specified files, quantity, territory and term; unused files returned/deleted.
2. **Quality/accessibility:** physical proof and legibility/reachability checks before manufacturing; distinguish armies without color; verify 64 pieces, board geometry, readable rules, durable markers, safe materials and clear customer support.
3. **Finance:** dated, itemized written quote for BOM, setup, samples, freight, tariffs, duties, tax, payment fees, replacements, fulfillment, refunds and delivery dates. Reconcile cost in one currency and period.
4. **Partner share:** auditable sales ledger, reporting cadence, defined allowed deductions, cap, end date, no cross-collateralization with other projects, and explicit final settlement.
5. **Performance bonus:** measurable sell-through, net of refunds, and a maximum payout; no promised guaranteed sales.
6. **Risk:** responsibility for defects, consumer protection, product liability, safety compliance, shipping loss, cancellation, unsold units, chargebacks and delayed production.
7. **Contributor rights:** no assumption that the existing illustrative 10% adjusted-gross contributor waterfall or unrestricted sponsorship allocation is a signed obligation; any approved compensation must be modeled separately and checked for legal/benefit effects.
8. **Approval:** no public preorder, money collection, order, signature, rights transfer or production merge before owner approval, documented costed BOM, playable proof and independent legal/accounting review as appropriate.
