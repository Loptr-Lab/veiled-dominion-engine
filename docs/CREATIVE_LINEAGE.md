# Veiled Dominion Creative Lineage

## Classification

**Original game IP · primary creative case · current rules authority: this repository**

Veiled Dominion has a creative history. Earlier stories and prototypes document recurring questions about power, restraint, identity, responsibility, loss, and collective survival. This record preserves that history without importing third-party expression or allowing archive materials to override current canon.

## Lineage map

| Earlier work | Contribution to the practice | Veiled Dominion boundary |
|---|---|---|

## Transfer protocol

Before a historical concept enters Veiled Dominion:

1. Express it in franchise-neutral, mechanically testable language.
2. Identify the player experience it should create.
3. Check it against the invariant: **Power becomes mastery through restraint.**
4. Classify it as narrative canon, game canon, adaptation, experimental, historical/archive, or unresolved.
5. Record the decision in the GDD, rulebook, ADR, or other appropriate authority.
6. Add implementation tests when behavior changes.
7. Preserve provenance without copying third-party protected expression.

## What the history does not prove

- It does not prove every current mechanic was fully designed in an early story.
- It does not make all projects one literal continuity.
- It does not grant rights in third-party franchises, music, images, performances, or names.
- It does not supersede `docs/CANON_AND_CONTINUITY.md`.
