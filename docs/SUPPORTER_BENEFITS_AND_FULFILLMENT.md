Patreon supports the technical development stream. Buy Me a Coffee / Urban Alien Adventures supports the narrative and worldbuilding stream. Discord is shared infrastructure; the Phase 1 game remains the earned playtester path.

## Rules
- Publish the exact Discord role and access duration for every qualifying tier.
- One-time support does not create permanent membership unless expressly promised.
- A short grace period may address payment or synchronization failures.
- Support does not buy canon, ownership, approvals, contribution acceptance, or playtest victory.
- Recognition is optional and requires the supporter's preferred public name.
- Benefits must be accessible or have an equivalent alternative.

## Service benefits
Any call must state duration, booking method, availability, expiration, rescheduling, cancellation, no-show, and whether it is advice, consultation, or a general project conversation. Casual tiers should not promise an unlimited or hard service-level agreement.

## Credits and producer language
Use "supporter" unless a written agreement defines a production role. "Executive Producer" and "shaping the trajectory" require legal review because they may imply credit, authority, ownership, or approval rights.

## Physical benefits
Before sale, document the item, rights, production cost, shipping regions, taxes, estimated delivery, substitutions, refunds, lost packages, address retention, and accessibility of the purchase path.
