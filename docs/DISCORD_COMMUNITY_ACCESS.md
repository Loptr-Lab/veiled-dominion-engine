# Discord Community Access

Discord is an interactive front door for community, narrative presentation, and playtesting. GitHub is the permanent record for rules, bugs, accepted variants, and technical decisions.

## Entry paths
| Path | Role | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public invite | Lobby, then Person of Interest | Rules, warnings, project orientation, lobby, public discussion |
| Buy Me a Coffee qualifying membership | Narrative Supporter | Story-facing updates and stated supporter benefits |
| Patreon qualifying membership, once configured | Technical Supporter | Engineering and documentation updates |
| Phase 1 game win code | Playtester | Early-playtest rooms, session reporting, applicable tables |
| Verified operations team | Case Liaison / Detective Liaison / project team | Moderation and project operations |

Payment does not automatically grant Playtester. Supporters may separately earn or receive a documented playtest invitation. Public rules, open-source contribution, and ordinary bug reporting remain available without payment.

## Existing channel map
- Intake & Forensics: onboarding, warnings, rules, about, how-to-play, lobby, intake.
- Autopsy Slab: team, credits, contribution, GitHub, development log.
- Evidence Locker: art/lore and narrative work; not rules authority.

- Variant Characters: historical exhibitions; label third-party references.
- Voice Rooms: opt-in sessions with advance recording notice and consent.

## Welcome message
Welcome to the Daddy's Little Mortis / Veiled Dominion playtest lab. This server began as the Violet's Revenge community and preserves that history while supporting variants, narrative experiments, open development, and structured playtesting.

Start by reading content-warning and server-rules, then visit how-to-play and the-lobby. Open-source-github links to the canonical technical record.

Supporter access may include updates, previews, exhibitions, and invitations. It does not purchase ownership, creative control, guaranteed inclusion, legal services, or permission to use third-party IP. Playtesting is opt-in. Voice sessions are not recorded without advance notice and participant consent.

## Support and access message
Patreon and Buy Me a Coffee are different doors into one project. Patreon is technical; Buy Me a Coffee is narrative. Equivalent benefits should use equivalent access even when tier names differ. Do not post receipts, addresses, account credentials, or payment identifiers. Use a private support ticket with platform, Discord username, membership level, and approximate join date.

## Bot and data rules
Use minimum permissions: view channels, send messages, embed links, read history, and required thread actions. Never grant Administrator solely for integration. Store tokens outside repositories. Record session IDs and rules versions, not unnecessary personal data. Publish a retention period before collecting transcripts or recordings.
