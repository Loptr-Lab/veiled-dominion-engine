## 1.0 Concept Overview

A 1:1 mechanical reskin of *Veiled Dominion* mapped onto Bungie’s core Light vs. Dark (Paracausal) philosophy. It translates the fundamental metaphysical rules of the Destiny universe into a turn-based strategy framework.

---

## 2.0 Lore Mapping

* **Rebirth (The Daughter) ➔ The Traveler / The Light:** Radiant, capable of resurrecting units (Soul Reservoir), but capable of blinding/burning those who enter without a buffer (Radius of Ruin).
* **Death (The Mentor) ➔ The Deep / The Witness (The Final Shape):** Represents ultimate stillness. The Sanctuary mechanic represents "The Resonance of the Deep"—a place of absolute peace where Light radiation cannot reach.

---

## 3.0 Piece & Mechanic Mapping

### 3.1 Core Mechanics
* **Radius of Ruin ➔ "Paracausal Feedback" / "Light Leak":** Units in the Traveler's radius lose abilities and move as basic Thralls (Pawns).
* **Mentor's Sanctuary ➔ "The Resonance of the Deep":** Units adjacent to the Witness/Deep piece are immune to Paracausal Feedback.
* **Martyr’s Boon ➔ "The Last Word" / "Hive Sword Logic":** Voluntarily sacrificing a piece to temporarily suppress the Traveler’s Light for one turn.
* **Soul Reservoir ➔ "Ghost Resurrection":** Unlocks movement boosts through occupied spaces for accumulated Graveyard count.
* **Mercy Victory ➔ "The Perfect Shape":** Winning through position and coordination without taking captures.

### 3.2 Factions & Hierarchy
* **Quadrant Factions:** The Vanguard (Light), Disciple/Pyramid Fleet (Darkness), Hive/Witness (Entities), Fallen/Cabal (Neutral/Mortal).
* **Piece Mapping:** Leader (Commander), Rebirth (Traveler), Death (Witness), Rooks (Titans), Bishops (Warlocks), Knights (Hunters), Pawns (Ghosts/Thralls).
