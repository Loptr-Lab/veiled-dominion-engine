## 1.0 Concept Overview

A premium, abstracted reskin of *Veiled Dominion* inspired by Josef Hartwig’s 1924 Bauhaus Chess Set. This variant strips away the figurative "Father/Daughter" narrative to focus entirely on the pure mechanics of light, space, and geometric function. Designed for the high-end art, design, and architecture collector market.

---

## 2.0 Design Philosophy: "Form is Mechanic"

A piece’s physical shape dictates its movement, and dynamically changes to reflect its mechanical state:

* **Death Faction (The Absolutes):** Machined, heavy, geometric solids coated in Musou Black or machined from solid black obsidian. They represent immovable, unyielding logic.
* **Rebirth Faction (The Radiants):** The exact same geometric shapes, milled from optical-grade, crystal-clear resin with internal LED cores.

---

## 3.0 Piece Dictionary (Geometry Mapping)

* **The Tower (Rook):** A perfect Cube. Represents blunt, vertical force.
* **The Bishop:** A conjoined dual-cone (an hourglass or X-shape). Represents diagonal splitting.
* **The Knight:** A literal, extruded L-shape. Represents the unconventional angle.
* **The Pawn:** A simple, short Cylinder.
* **Death (The Mentor):** A massive, perfectly smooth Sphere. (A void with no edges to catch light).
* **Rebirth (The Daughter):** A massive, perfect Sphere. (A point of light radiating equally in all directions, visualizing the 1-square "Radius of Ruin").

---

## 4.0 Material Science & The "Silhouette Match"

If you place a translucent Rebirth Bishop next to a Musou Black Death Bishop, their physical silhouettes are 100% identical. You cannot tell them apart from a top-down view or by their shadow. Factions are distinguished solely by how they interact with light: Death devours light; Rebirth diffuses it. The board surface is polished black optical glass.

---

## 5.0 UX Visualization: Geometry Collapsing Animation

Solves tracking status effects visually in digital prototypes:

* When a complex shape (e.g., the X-shaped Bishop) enters the Daughter's glowing radius (*Radius of Ruin*), the model smoothly animates and collapses into a basic, featureless Cylinder (a Pawn).
* When it leaves the radius, the Cylinder expands back into its original shape.
* The board’s geometry physically shifts based on game state, eliminating the need for UI token markers.
