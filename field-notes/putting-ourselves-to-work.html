Putting Ourselves to Work: From Disability Benefits to Accessible Entrepreneurship

Studio homeField NotesTraining

Founding field note · November 25, 2026

What if the next step toward economic independence is not waiting for a workplace to become accessible, but building work that can accommodate the people doing it?

Putting ourselves to work

Disability benefits provide essential stability. They should not be treated as evidence of a lack of ambition, skill or capacity to contribute. Loptr Lab explores how accessible creative technology, flexible work and open development can become a route toward sustainable paid work. The goal is not to work at any cost, but to build a livelihood that respects health, access needs and human dignity.

Start with the work already underway

We are building a portfolio rather than waiting for an opportunity to demonstrate our abilities. Play It Forward explores music discovery, interactive storytelling and fan participation. Duet provides a game-development testing pathway. Loptr Lab Training turns practical work into learning evidence. PIXIE Holdings supports assessment of sustainable operations. These are developing projects, not proof of revenue, completed client contracts or independently verified outcomes.

From capability to paid work

Open development can demonstrate research, systems architecture, design, implementation and maintenance. A prospective engagement should define deliverables, accessibility requirements, costs, compensation, intellectual property, acceptance criteria and a realistic workload before work begins. A paid discovery pilot is a possible next step, not an announced partnership. Existing reusable infrastructure remains distinct from commissioned deliverables.

Record hours, actual expenses, completed deliverables, testing results, and client outcomes. Separate estimated agency replacement cost from actual revenue and business valuation. Do not present artist growth as a result of our work without evidence.

Economic security during the transition

SSI and SSDI have different work and reporting rules. Self-employment activity, earnings, resources and hours may matter depending on the program. Housing assistance and healthcare have separate requirements. Before making commitments, identify applicable benefits, report work as required, and obtain individualized benefits counseling. Do not assume that a business entity, unpaid activity or deferred payment protects eligibility.

Explore official Social Security work incentives, Ticket to Work, and Disability Hub MN. An approved Plan to Achieve Self-Support may be relevant; eligibility is individual. This article is a studio research statement, not benefits, legal or tax advice. Individual records and assessments remain private.

Designing work around people

Accessible technology is part of the working environment, not a reward for productivity. Flexible deadlines, device-compatible workflows, readable interfaces, clear scope and the ability to stop are design requirements. Paid assignments should not depend on unpaid speculative labor or the disclosure of disability information.

Use the existing independent-work pathway to turn capability into a bounded next action. Made-Sick remains the broader resource and lived-experience publication.

What we will measure

Document delivery quality, actual cost, accessibility testing, sustainable workload, paid opportunities and lessons learned. The public record will distinguish implemented work from proposals, and verified outcomes from assumptions. Personal benefits and financial records will not be published.

Our objective is to create meaningful work that can sustain itself without sacrificing the people it is intended to serve.
