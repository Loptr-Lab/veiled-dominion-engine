One Universe, Many Entrances: Designing Transmedia Systems Without Losing Continuity

Studio homeField NotesTraining

Studio case study · November 25, 2026

Transmedia is not merely storytelling across platforms. It requires continuity across independently functioning creative systems, without confusing a shared universe with shared ownership or a shared deployment.

One universe is not one codebase

Urban Alien Adventures provides a narrative context; Veiled Dominion governs its four-player game rules, while Duet is a separate two-player experiment. Play It Forward is an independent music-led cultural discovery project, not evidence of artist participation or common canon. Training turns these practices into transferable learning. PIXIE supports sourced guidance and continuity; it cannot approve canon, grant permission or substitute for human consent.

See the ecosystem directory, the existing project bible and Veiled Dominion canon policy. These retain their own authority; this article does not change them.

Boundaries are an architectural requirement

Source authority
Identify owning repository, editable source versus generated output, version and responsible maintainer.
Narrative authority
Distinguish original work, adaptations, experimental mechanics, external references and approved canon.
Rights and consent
Record authorship, licenses, permitted use and approvals separately; an invitation or public reference does not establish collaboration.
Public navigation
Give each entrance one purpose; maintain stable explicit destination links and document legacy redirects.
Shared services
Reuse infrastructure through defined interfaces without making one project's availability a prerequisite for unrelated work.
Release evidence
Separate committed source, CI, deployed artifact, live URL checks and independent human accessibility acceptance.

Research foundation

These works inform the research questions, rather than validate this particular implementation.

  • Henry Jenkins (2007), Transmedia Storytelling 101: coordination and distinct contributions across media.
  • Linda Hutcheon with Siobhan O'Flynn, A Theory of Adaptation, second edition: adaptation across media; a starting point for examining creative transformations.
  • David L. Parnas (1972), On the Criteria To Be Used in Decomposing Systems into Modules: modular decomposition and the separation of changeable design decisions.
  • W3C (2008), Cool URIs for the Semantic Web: stable, manageable identifiers. Apply the principle to public navigation without implying that this studio implements Semantic Web standards.

Human cost and sustainable entrepreneurship

Repeated repairs consume time, attention, accessibility capacity and money. A small studio needs a reliable path from creative intent to public experience. Measure repeated repair hours, broken links, actual hosting cost, time to restore service, and evidence of successful device use. No measured savings or verified improvement is claimed yet.

This supports Putting Ourselves to Work and the Designing Humanity into Technology research framework. The case is a practice-research observation, not a completed study or a claim of academic enrollment.

From silent coupling to executable checks

Credit: Pietro Impagliazzo, Petrus Agentic Coupling System (PACS), MIT. The specification identifies distant dependencies that fail silently. Our initial pilot links the shared breadcrumb selector to a registry entry and an executable check in existing CI. It uses no upstream CLI or Claude hooks. A reported registry consultation is not proof of compliance; test results and deployed behavior remain distinct. Training exercise: reintroduce the old metadata ID in a disposable test copy and observe the regression check reject it. Pilot acceptance remains pending.

Practical acceptance

For each release, check the owning source, generated output, canonical destination, redirect behavior, return links and actual public response. Record the commit and observation time. Keep iPad, keyboard and VoiceOver results separate from automated checks. An unverified link remains unverified even if CI is green. Preserve rollback and permissions; do not change game rules or hosting as a side effect of navigation repair.
