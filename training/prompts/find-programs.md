# Prompt: find programs

> Find programs in **[jurisdiction]** that may relate to **[vocational goal or documented need]**. Start with current official government, educational, or program-owner sources. For each candidate, provide its exact name, responsible authority, official link, source verification date, stated purpose, and how a person applies or asks a question. Separate confirmed facts from possible relevance. Mark unconfirmed fit or availability as **unverified**. Do not predict eligibility or approval; identify the human decision-maker.
