# Prompt: adapt research to another jurisdiction

> Adapt this research method—not its conclusions—from **[current jurisdiction]** to **[new jurisdiction]** for **[question]**. Identify the correct levels of authority and current official sources in the new jurisdiction. Provide links and verification dates, distinguish confirmed facts from uncertainty, and mark unsupported mappings **unverified**. Do not assume that program names, eligibility, funding, deadlines, appeals, or decision-makers transfer between jurisdictions.
