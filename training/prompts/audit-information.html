Prompt: audit existing information

Audit the following material for outdated facts, unsupported assumptions, duplicated guidance, privacy risk, and claims that only a human authority can make: [material]. Check current authoritative sources and link them with verification dates. Classify each claim as confirmed, contradicted, uncertain, or unverified. Identify volatile facts that should move into dated research notes. Preserve substantive content unless you can state why it is superseded or intentionally retired.
