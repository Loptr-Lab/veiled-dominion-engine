AI research prompts

Use AI research with human oversight

These prompts are designed to make AI research more reliable, not to replace the agency or person who makes the decision. Each prompt requires current authoritative sources, direct links, verification dates, separation of confirmed facts from uncertainty, and the word unverified instead of a guess.

  • Safe research practices
  • Find programs
  • Verify eligibility information
  • Research technology
  • Compare costs and alternatives
  • Build a request
  • Audit existing information
  • Adapt research to another jurisdiction

Never paste credentials, government identifiers, case numbers, private correspondence, medical records, benefit letters, or confidential documents into a research prompt.
