Prompt: verify eligibility information

Audit the claim [claim] for [program and jurisdiction]. Use current authoritative sources, link to the controlling or most direct source, and state the date verified. Quote only the minimum language needed. Distinguish general eligibility rules from an individual decision, identify exceptions or missing facts, and mark unsupported claims as unverified. State who has authority to decide and prepare concise questions for that person. Do not request personal identifiers or confidential records.
