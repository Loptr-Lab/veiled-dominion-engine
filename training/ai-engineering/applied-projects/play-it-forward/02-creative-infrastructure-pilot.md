# Experiment 02: Creative Infrastructure Audit

**Status:** proposed protocol, not yet run.  
**Purpose:** test whether existing assets, channels and relationships can create a more accessible, discoverable and sustainable creator pathway without unnecessary platform expansion.

## Research question

What support does a small creator need to turn existing creative work into discoverable, rights-aware, accessible offers—and can Loptr Lab make that process easier without adding platforms, accounts or overhead that the evidence does not justify?

This applies lessons from the [HyperDot / Tribe Games / GLITCH case study](../../case-studies/hyperdot-glitch.md). It does not claim that Loptr Lab has the same resources or commercial results.

## Guardrails

- Start with existing projects, assets and channels. No new platform or account by default.
- Use only assets with documented rights and permissions.
- No publishing, outreach, spending, enrollment or production-system changes without explicit human approval.
- No private audience data or sensitive personal information.
- Include disabled participants in accessibility review where feasible, with consent, clear scope, attribution preferences and compensation agreed in advance.
- Do not present proposals, impressions or traffic as revenue or validated demand.

## Baseline

1. Choose one existing creative project and one defined audience task.
2. Record current channels, available audience/reach metrics and measurement period.
3. Inventory assets, rights, permissions, formats and accessibility status.
4. Record creator time, recurring costs and any existing revenue or conversion evidence.
5. Define a falsifiable success criterion and stop condition before intervention.

## Intervention

1. Improve one discovery route on an existing owned page or channel.
2. Make the next action clear: learn, view, listen, attend, buy, contribute or request information.
3. Check keyboard use, text alternatives, captions/transcripts, contrast, mobile layout and reduced motion.
4. Use one rights-cleared creative asset to demonstrate a path from discovery to a meaningful offer.
5. Record human edits, tools, costs, time and decisions. Review AI output before use.

## Measures

| Dimension | Measure | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery | Qualified visits or completed discovery actions against baseline | Existing analytics or dated manual counts |
| Accessibility | Critical barriers found/resolved; task completion with reviewer feedback | Test notes and consented feedback |
| Rights | Share of published assets with documented permissions/provenance | Asset register |
| Labor | Creator hours per deliverable; rework and handoff time | Time log |
| Economics | Direct costs, gross receipts, refunds and contribution margin where available | Actual voluntary records; no inferred sales |
| Reuse | Assets, templates or workflows safely reused | Versioned artifacts and permission scope |

## Stop / continue rules

- **Stop** if rights are unclear, critical barriers remain, a new platform is required without demonstrated benefit, or costs/obligations exceed approved limits.
- **Revise** if discovery improves but the next action is confusing, labor rises without corresponding benefit, or accessibility review identifies barriers.
- **Continue cautiously** only if the intervention improves a predeclared measure without violating rights, consent, accessibility or budget constraints.

A null or negative result is valid. Do not generalize from a small pilot.

## Reporting

Publish the question, dates, baseline, intervention, measures, actual costs, limitations, corrections and next decision. Separate observed data from interpretation. Do not identify participants without permission.
