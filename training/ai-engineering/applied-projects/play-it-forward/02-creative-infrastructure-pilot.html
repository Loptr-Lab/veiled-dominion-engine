PLAY IT FORWARD / FIELD EXPERIMENT 02

Creative Infrastructure Audit

Can existing assets, channels and relationships create a more accessible and sustainable path for a creator?

Research question

What support does a small creator need to turn existing creative work into discoverable, rights-aware, accessible offers—and can Loptr Lab make that process easier without adding platforms, accounts or overhead that the evidence does not justify?

This experiment applies lessons from the HyperDot / Tribe Games / GLITCH case study. It is not a claim that our ecosystem has the same resources or commercial results.

Read the case study · Read the decision brief

Guardrails

  • Start with existing projects, assets and channels. No new platform or account by default.
  • Use only assets with documented rights and permissions. Keep provenance and human authorship visible.
  • Do not publish, contact, spend, enroll or change production systems without explicit human approval.
  • Do not use private audience data or sensitive personal information.
  • Include disabled participants in accessibility review where feasible, with consent, clear scope, attribution preferences and compensation arrangements agreed in advance.
  • Do not represent proposals, impressions or traffic as revenue or validated demand.

Baseline before intervention

  1. Choose one existing creative project and one defined audience task.
  2. Record current channels, audience/reach metrics that are already available, and the period measured.
  3. Inventory usable assets, ownership, licenses, permissions, formats and accessibility status.
  4. Record creator time, recurring costs and any existing revenue or conversion evidence.
  5. Write a falsifiable success criterion and a stop condition before doing the work.

Intervention

  1. Improve one discovery route on an existing owned page or channel.
  2. Make the next action clear: learn, view, listen, attend, buy, contribute or request information.
  3. Check keyboard use, text alternatives, captions/transcripts, contrast, mobile layout and reduced-motion needs.
  4. Use one rights-cleared creative asset to demonstrate a pathway from discovery to a meaningful offer.
  5. Record every human edit, tool, cost, time and decision. AI output must be reviewed before use.

Measures

DimensionMeasureEvidence
DiscoveryQualified visits or completed discovery actions against baselineExisting analytics or manual counts with dates and definitions
AccessibilityCritical barriers found and resolved; task completion with reviewer feedbackTest notes, assistive-technology checks and consented feedback
RightsShare of published assets with documented permissions/provenanceAsset register and license/permission references
LaborCreator hours per deliverable; rework and handoff timeTime log with task definitions
EconomicsDirect costs, gross receipts, refunds and contribution margin where availableActual records supplied voluntarily; no inferred sales
ReuseAssets, templates or workflows safely reusedVersioned artifacts and documented permission scope

Stop / continue rules

  • Stop if rights are unclear, accessibility barriers remain critical, the task requires a new platform without a demonstrated benefit, or the work creates costs or obligations the creator did not approve.
  • Revise if discovery improves but the next action is confusing, labor increases without a corresponding benefit, or reviewers identify a meaningful access barrier.
  • Continue cautiously only if the intervention improves a predeclared measure without violating rights, consent, accessibility or budget constraints.

A null or negative result is a valid result. Do not convert a small pilot into a broad market claim.

Review and reporting

Publish a short result record containing the question, dates, baseline, intervention, measures, actual costs, limitations, corrections and next decision. Separate observed data from interpretation. Do not identify participants without permission.

Return findings to the case-study collection and the AI Engineering course.

Use what exists. Measure what changes. Preserve agency, rights and access.
