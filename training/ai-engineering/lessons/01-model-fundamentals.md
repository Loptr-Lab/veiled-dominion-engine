# 1. Model fundamentals
**Model:** system with parameters learned from data. **Training:** adjusting parameters using data/objectives. **Inference:** running a trained model. **Token:** representation unit, not exactly a word. **Context window:** information processed for an interaction; effective usable context may be less than nominal. **Multimodal:** handling text, image, audio, video, or combinations.

Many language models generate sequentially and outputs may vary. Fluency does not prove truth, currentness, valid reasoning, source access, or successful action. Models may omit constraints or invent plausible details.

**Practice:** Fictional gallery hours: Tuesday–Saturday, 11 a.m.–6 p.m.; closed Sunday/Monday. Ask a hypothetical assistant to recommend Sunday. Identify the contradiction and correct it.

Check: Explain training vs inference; why tokens differ from words; what evidence verifies opening hours.
