Key concepts

Model
A system with parameters learned from data.
Training
Adjusting parameters using data and objectives.
Inference
Running a trained model.
Token
A representation unit—not exactly a word.
Context window
Information processed for an interaction; effective usable context may be less than nominal.
Multimodal
Handling text, image, audio, video, or combinations.

Many language models generate sequentially and outputs may vary. Fluency does not prove truth, currentness, valid reasoning, source access, or successful action. Models may omit constraints or invent plausible details.

Practice

Fictional gallery hours: Tuesday–Saturday, 11 a.m.–6 p.m.; closed Sunday/Monday. Ask a hypothetical assistant to recommend Sunday. Identify the contradiction and correct it.
