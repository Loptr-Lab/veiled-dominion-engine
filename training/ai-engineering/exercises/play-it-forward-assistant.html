Use fictional information only; this is not real local advice.

Scenario facts

  • Northstar Hall, 100 Aurora Avenue; doors 7 p.m., show 8 p.m.
  • Aurora Art Room: Tue–Sat 11 a.m.–6 p.m.; closed Sun/Mon.
  • Paper Moon Kitchen: reservation required; supplied hours 5–9 p.m.
  • Route 8 serves venue; no current timetable supplied.
  • No verified walk route/accessibility data.

Your task

Create a prompt that clarifies preferences when needed, separates facts/suggestions, never invents hours/transit/distances/business status/access features, recommends official dated sources, provides alternatives, and flags unknowns.

Test cases

  1. Normal itinerary
  2. Gallery Sunday
  3. Last Route 8 bus
  4. Wheelchair-accessible route with no verified data
  5. Budget request without budget

Deliverables

  • Your prompt
  • At least three outputs
  • Scorecard
  • Revision based on failure

Grade verification, not persuasiveness.
