# Public-interest reviewer map

**Purpose:** identify people and organizations whose public professional remit makes them appropriate to validate this case study or its proposed experiment.

**This is not a dossier on private individuals.** The Training repository is public. This map therefore contains only public organizational roles, public work and transparent review questions. It does not store personal contact details, private relationships, personal vulnerabilities, private correspondence, speculative allegations or coercive “leverage.” Do not infer endorsement from inclusion.

## First review sequence

1. Ask the creator for factual correction before treating the narrative as settled.
2. Ask the historical publisher/community source to distinguish documented support from interpretation.
3. Ask accessibility specialists and disabled-player advocates to review the accessibility account and experiment safeguards.
4. Ask industry researchers to validate the historical survey interpretation and point to newer evidence.
5. Ask publishers, educators and platform teams targeted questions only after the case has been corrected.

## Reviewer qualification record

Before inviting anyone, verify from public sources:
- current professional role and organizational affiliation;
- relevant experience with the question being reviewed;
- whether the person has decision authority, subject expertise or lived/practice experience relevant to the review;
- any publicly disclosed commercial or institutional interest that could affect interpretation;
- the exact, bounded request and what information they will receive.

Do not rank a person by rumor, private life, perceived vulnerability or imagined influence. A reviewer may be both a potential ally and a commercial stakeholder. Record the interest transparently.

## Safe outreach contract

- One specific request per invitation; no implied endorsement.
- No unsolicited bulk outreach or automated contact.
- No request for confidential contracts, private financial records or unpublished participant data.
- Review may be declined or withdrawn without penalty.
- Agree in writing on attribution and whether comments may be quoted.
- No unpaid labor is presumed. Any commissioned work requires a separate scope and compensation agreement.
- Do not publish a reviewer's name, response or affiliation as an endorsement without permission.

## Status vocabulary

- **Unresearched:** remit not yet checked.
- **Qualified:** public remit matches a specific review question.
- **Invited:** a bounded request was sent.
- **Accepted:** reviewer agreed to the defined review.
- **Completed:** comments received and disposition recorded.
- **Declined / no response:** no further assumptions or repeated pressure.
