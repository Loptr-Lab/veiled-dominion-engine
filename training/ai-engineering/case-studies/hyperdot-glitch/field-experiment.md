# PLAY IT FORWARD field experiment: creator infrastructure

**Status:** Proposed protocol; no experiment results claimed.

## Question

Can existing Loptr Lab assets provide a measurable, lower-friction pathway from cultural discovery to meaningful participation or creator support, while respecting rights, accessibility, consent, and user control?

## Hypothesis

A connected pathway (discover → contextualize → choose → participate → support) may reduce friction compared with isolated links or promotional content. This remains a hypothesis until tested.

## Scope guardrails

- Optimize current channels and assets; no new platform/account without evidence and explicit approval.
- Do not scrape or automate engagement in ways that violate platform rules.
- No posting, contacting, purchasing, publishing, or enrolling users without explicit authorization.
- Use only source material and images with appropriate rights/permissions.
- Make accessibility and privacy part of the design and evaluation.
- Distinguish a click, visit, meaningful action, creator benefit, and revenue; they are not interchangeable.

## Baseline

Before experiment, record:
- Existing page/channel, source, date range, current traffic/engagement if available.
- Current pathway and where users abandon (only with valid analytics).
- Existing content assets and their rights/attribution.
- Costs and time spent by the creator.
- What is not measurable because analytics or attribution are unavailable.

## Proposed test

1. Select one existing cultural-discovery pathway in PLAY IT FORWARD.
2. Use the research-backed case study to provide context, not as an endorsement or sales claim.
3. Offer a small set of meaningful, rights-aware next actions: learn, explore source, participate, or support.
4. Keep the choice voluntary and explain destination/impact honestly.
5. Run a defined, small test against the existing baseline. Do not create a new channel.
6. Review results before expanding.

## Measures

| Metric | Definition | Interpretation limit |
|---|---|---|
| Qualified discovery | Visit from a documented relevant source | Does not prove interest or benefit |
| Path completion | User reaches a chosen next-step destination | Does not prove action there |
| Meaningful action | Voluntary action measurable with permission | Define action before test |
| Creator-support conversion | Verified support transaction, if accessible | Never infer from clicks |
| Accessibility issues | Documented barriers found in testing | Small sample is not universal |
| Creator effort | Hours and direct costs | Include maintenance burden |
| Rights/privacy incidents | Any unauthorized use, disclosure, or tracking | Stop and remediate |

## Decision criteria (set before launch)

- **Continue:** evidence of useful participation and acceptable creator effort; no unresolved rights/privacy/accessibility issue.
- **Revise:** users encounter friction, results are inconclusive, or effort is disproportionate.
- **Stop:** legal/rights/privacy concern, unsafe practice, misleading claims, or no plausible benefit after a bounded test.

Set numerical thresholds only after baseline and available measurement are known; do not retrofit success criteria to the results.

## Review and publication

Publish a concise outcome with methods, limitations, negative findings, and links to source evidence. Do not identify participants without consent. A result should be reproducible enough for a reviewer to understand what was tested and what remains unknown.
