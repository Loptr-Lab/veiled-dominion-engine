# Review map: independent-game infrastructure

**Internal research aid.** This is a list of roles and public organizations to investigate, not an outreach list, endorsement list, or dossier on private individuals. Verify current names, affiliations, decision authority, and contact routes before outreach.

## First validation circle

1. **Charles McGregor / Tribe Games** — factual creator-history review; what support was useful or missing.
2. **GLITCH's historical Minnesota leadership / archive custodians** — reconstruct the documented publishing and community model. Confirm organizational continuity before attributing historical work to current entities.
3. **IGDA Game Accessibility SIG** — review accessibility framing and research practice.
4. **AbleGamers** — review whether the proposed participatory process gives disabled players meaningful input.
5. **IGDA advocacy / developer-sustainability leadership** — review the labor, discoverability and sustainability framing.

## Decision-maker classes

| Class | Authority / expertise | Review question |
|---|---|---|
| Independent publisher/label | Publishing, marketing, distribution, production support | Which supports are repeatable and economically viable? |
| Solo/small-studio creator | Lived production constraints | Does our model reduce real workload or merely add process? |
| Accessibility advocate/researcher | Participatory methods and accessibility | Are claims accurate; who is involved and compensated? |
| Industry association | Shared standards and advocacy | What systemic issue can the evidence support? |
| Arts/workforce funder | Grants, programs, capacity building | Is there a testable, responsibly scoped public benefit? |
| Educator/researcher | Curriculum, methods, evaluation | Can this be replicated and assessed? |
| Platform/distribution team | Discovery, access metadata, distribution | What platform-level change could improve outcomes? |

## Ethical influence ledger (the “Black Book” concept)

Record only public professional information relevant to a proposed collaboration or review:

- Name and verified public role/organization
- Domain and actual decision authority
- Public evidence of priorities/programs
- Relevant connection or prior public work
- Proposed value exchange and specific request
- Public professional contact route, if explicitly published
- Verification date, source, confidence, and correction history
- Status: researched / qualified / approached with consent / responded / engaged

**Do not record:** private contact details, family or personal vulnerabilities, speculative motives, sensitive traits, rumors, coercive leverage, or information gathered to intimidate or manipulate. The objective is informed, respectful engagement with institutions and decision-makers—not targeting individuals.

## Approach sequence

1. Creator and historical-community fact-check.
2. Accessibility and developer-sustainability review.
3. Small-publisher/operator review.
4. Funders and educators after claims and experiment design are strengthened.
5. Platform conversations only when the field experiment demonstrates a concrete platform-level issue.

No outreach is authorized by this document. Each approach should be a transparent review request with the relevant evidence attached and a clear opt-out.
