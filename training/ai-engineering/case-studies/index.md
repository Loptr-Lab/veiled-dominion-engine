## Evidence rules

- A public event listing does not prove an individual attended or met another attendee.
- A creator's retrospective is first-person testimony, not an independent audit of a publisher's finances.
- Public prices, review counts and awards do not reveal development budgets, sales, profit or creator income.
- Survey respondents are not a census of the whole industry.
- Interpretations and proposed experiments are labeled as such.
- Reviewer research uses public professional information only. No private contact details, personal vulnerabilities, surveillance, coercion or speculative allegations belong here.
