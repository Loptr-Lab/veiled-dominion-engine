ScreenWeaver — AI Video Prompts Like a Director

Open the ScreenWeaver course

  • Iterating on generations and diagnosing failures

Suggested learning sequence

  1. Study the free ScreenWeaver material and note the directing concepts that apply across video models.
  2. Make a short scene using a storyboard, shot list and consistent visual references.
  3. Keep a generation log: prompt, model/version, settings, result, failure and revision.
  4. Evaluate whether you need a paid course's feedback, structure or professional community before purchasing.

Reusable directing prompt structure

For each shot, define: subject + action + setting + framing/camera + motion + lighting/style + emotional intent + continuity constraints + duration. Treat this as a checklist, not magic syntax; adapt to the model and test one change at a time.

Use references you have rights to use, consider consent and likeness rights, and review platform terms, provenance and disclosure requirements before publishing generated work.
