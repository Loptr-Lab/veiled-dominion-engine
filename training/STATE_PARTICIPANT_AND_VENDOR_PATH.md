# Participant and Vendor Path for Other States

_This is a routing guide, not a statement that Loptr Lab is approved in any state._

State vocational-rehabilitation and workforce systems use different names, service categories, contracts, provider lists, and authorization rules. Keep two processes separate.

## Path A — participant requests individual review

1. Find the official state vocational-rehabilitation agency through the state government website.
2. Apply or request an intake using the agency's official process.
3. Develop or update the participant's employment plan with the assigned counselor.
4. Provide the Loptr Lab curriculum, expected outcomes, schedule, accessibility plan, delivery method, proposed cost, and instructor information.
5. Ask whether the agency may approve the activity individually, requires an approved provider, or can use another purchasing mechanism.
6. Obtain the determination and any authorization in writing before treating the activity as funded or referred.
7. Keep private case material outside GitHub and follow agency reporting instructions.

## Path B — agency considers Loptr Lab as a provider/vendor

A participant may ask an agency to review Loptr Lab, but a participant does not grant vendor status unless they independently hold documented agency authority.

Ask the agency:

- Which office approves vocational-rehabilitation service providers?
- Is there a vendor application, contract solicitation, cooperative agreement, or approved-provider list?
- Which proposed service category best matches this curriculum?
- Is there a documented need for the service?
- What legal-entity standing, tax registration, insurance, accessibility, nondiscrimination, privacy, cybersecurity, background-check, staff-qualification, pricing, reporting, invoicing, and audit requirements apply?
- May services be remote or interstate?
- Are participant wages required for projects, internships, evaluations, or work experiences?
- Can a qualified local provider sponsor or subcontract the training?
- What may Loptr Lab accurately say publicly while the review is pending?

## Evidence packet

Prepare only public or purpose-limited program information:

- program summary and employment-facing outcomes;
- curriculum and estimated hours;
- accessibility and accommodation process;
- instructor qualifications;
- service-delivery and supervision plan;
- pricing and cancellation terms;
- participant progress and outcome reporting;
- privacy, records, incident, complaint, and nonretaliation policies;
- entity, tax, insurance, and vendor records requested by the agency.

Do not include a participant's medical, benefit, housing, financial, or identifying records in a general provider packet.

## Required public wording

Until an agency gives written approval:

> Loptr Lab is seeking agency review. Participation and funding are determined individually by the responsible agency. No approval, referral, payment, employment, or benefit outcome is guaranteed.

## Verification log

For every state, record:

`state | official agency | official URL | contact office | participant route | provider route | service category | verification date | reviewer | status | next action`

Allowed statuses:

- `RESEARCHED`
- `AGENCY CONTACTED`
- `IN REVIEW`
- `CHANGES REQUESTED`
- `APPROVED IN WRITING`
- `DECLINED`
- `EXPIRED / REVERIFY`

Never label a state `APPROVED` from participant interest, counselor discussion, a submitted application, or silence.
