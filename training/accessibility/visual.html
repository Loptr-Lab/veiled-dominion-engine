Visual access

Document the task and observable barrier: text size, contrast, glare, focus visibility, dense layout, color dependence, detail inspection, or eye fatigue. Required capabilities may include scalable text, reflow, high contrast, adjustable brightness, non-color cues, zoom that preserves context, and display positioning. Test the actual application; a hardware specification alone does not prove access.
