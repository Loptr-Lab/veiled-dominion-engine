Magnification

Test operating-system and application zoom at the participant's working scale. Check reflow, pointer tracking, focus visibility, context retention, dialogs, timelines, canvases, and multi-pane layouts. A larger display may help some workflows, but it is not a substitute for software that remains operable when magnified.
