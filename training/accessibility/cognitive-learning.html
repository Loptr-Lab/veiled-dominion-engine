Cognitive and learning access

Reduce avoidable memory, sequencing, naming, interruption, and context-switching burdens. Useful capabilities may include clear steps, persistent state, meaningful defaults, templates, checklists, searchable history, undo, reminders, transcription, plain-language explanations, and resumable workflows. The system should support autonomy rather than make decisions or disclose information on the participant's behalf.
