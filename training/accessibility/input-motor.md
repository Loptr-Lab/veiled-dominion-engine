# Input and motor access

Identify precision, reach, repetition, pressure, timing, posture, and fatigue barriers. Consider keyboard-only use, remapping, switch access, voice input, pointing alternatives, adjustable timing, automation, ergonomic positioning, and recovery from accidental activation. Evaluate sustained real tasks, not a brief demonstration.

Use the [adaptive creative-work planning exercise](../case-study/03-accessibility-requirements.html#adaptive-work) to connect these choices to a task, rights and support route.
