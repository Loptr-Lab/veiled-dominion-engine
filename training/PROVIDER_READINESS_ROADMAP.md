# Provider Readiness Roadmap

## Current position

Loptr Lab has a public training curriculum and contributor-safety materials. It is not currently represented here as a contracted Minnesota VRS Community Partner or an approved provider in another state.

The legal entity, contracting authority, insurance, service classification, staff qualifications, pricing, privacy controls, participant records, invoicing, and state-specific approval must be verified before Loptr Lab accepts agency-funded referrals.

## Gates

### Gate 1 — lawful contracting identity

- establish or restore an active legal entity in the appropriate jurisdiction;
- register any required assumed name;
- obtain and reconcile EIN, tax, banking, and ownership records;
- confirm who may sign contracts;
- create separate business accounting and payment controls;
- retain current formation and good-standing evidence.

**No provider application should imply that an inactive entity is active.**

### Gate 2 — define the service

Choose a narrow initial service. Document:

- target employment outcome;
- participant eligibility assumptions;
- curriculum, hours, delivery method, and prerequisites;
- accommodations and accessible formats;
- qualified instructor/supervisor;
- measurable deliverables and outcomes;
- boundaries among education, volunteer contribution, internship, employment, contracting, and self-employment.

The agency—not Loptr Lab alone—determines the purchasable service classification.

### Gate 3 — participant protections

Adopt:

- informed consent and participation terms;
- accommodation-request process;
- nondiscrimination and nonretaliation policy;
- complaint, incident, and appeal-routing process;
- safeguarding and professional-boundary rules;
- privacy, release-of-information, retention, breach-response, and deletion rules;
- least-privilege access and credential revocation;
- clear compensation and intellectual-property terms.

### Gate 4 — operational evidence

Prepare:

- attendance/service log;
- referral and authorization verification;
- individualized objective and accommodation record;
- progress and outcome report;
- direct-service time record where required;
- instructor qualification file;
- invoice support;
- participant exit and credential-revocation checklist;
- quality review and corrective-action log.

### Gate 5 — agency review

For Minnesota, contact VRS Community Partnerships using the current contact information on the [official VRS page](https://mn.gov/deed/job-seekers/disabilities/partners/contacts/).

Ask VRS to confirm:

- program need;
- appropriate service title;
- provider qualifications;
- contract and supplier requirements;
- rates and allowable costs;
- referral and prior-authorization workflow;
- required reports and invoices;
- wage rules;
- insurance, accessibility, privacy, records, monitoring, and audit requirements.

### Gate 6 — controlled pilot

Start only after the required written approval or contract is effective.

- accept only properly referred and authorized participants;
- verify each authorization before service;
- deliver only the authorized scope;
- protect private records;
- report and invoice exactly as required;
- distinguish participant outcomes from promises;
- pause if the authorization expires or material terms change.

## Public status labels

Use only:

- `DESIGNING`
- `DOCUMENTING`
- `AGENCY CONTACTED`
- `IN REVIEW`
- `CHANGES REQUESTED`
- `APPROVED IN WRITING`
- `CONTRACT ACTIVE`
- `PAUSED / EXPIRED`

A pull request, participant endorsement, counselor conversation, submitted application, or repository inclusion is not vendor approval.
