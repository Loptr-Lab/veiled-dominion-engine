YOUR ROUTE

Six steps from goal to request

  1. Identify your work. Name the vocational goal and the tasks the work requires.
  2. Identify barriers. Describe what makes those tasks harder.
  3. Define requirements. Translate tasks and barriers into technology and accessibility capabilities.
  4. Research funding. Find programs that may support the documented need.
  5. Verify current rules. Check official sources and ask the responsible agency.
  6. Prepare your request. Assemble documentation for a counselor, employer, or funder.
