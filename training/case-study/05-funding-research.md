# 5. Funding research

The participant identifies the relevant jurisdiction and researches programs through current official sources. General program descriptions are separated from an individual eligibility or authorization decision. Conflicts and missing facts are labeled **unverified**, and questions are prepared for the responsible counselor or agency.

Benefits, housing, business activity, and training-provider status are treated as separate authority questions rather than collapsed into one AI-generated conclusion.
