# Xbox development workstation: dated purchasing example

**Checked September 26, 2026 (US market).** These are planning ranges, not quotes or guaranteed prices. Recheck stock, seller, configuration, taxes, shipping, and return terms before any purchase or funding request. Use the [game development track](../tracks/game-development.md) to establish requirements first, and record actual offers in the [quote comparison](../templates/quote-comparison.md).

For a trustworthy purchase, compare **direct manufacturer stores** such as [Dell](https://www.dell.com/en-us/shop/desktop-computers/sr/desktops) or [Lenovo](https://www.lenovo.com/us/en/c/desktops/), and **Best Buy items sold by Best Buy**. Verify the seller line, exact CPU/GPU, installed RAM, SSD capacity, Windows x64, warranty, returns, and whether monitor/peripherals are included. A marketplace listing on a reputable site is not necessarily sold or warranted by that retailer. Seek two itemized, dated quotes for the same capability set.

## Stage the decision

1. Validate the chosen game engine and representative project on a Windows x64 PC. An iPad may connect remotely, but confirm that editor, debugger, sound, controller, and accessible input remain usable over that connection.
2. Choose 32 GB RAM and 1–2 TB NVMe as a comfortable planning target, then adjust based on measured workloads. Microsoft's hard GDK installation space figure is **30 GB**, not a complete project storage requirement. [Microsoft development PC requirements](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/gaming/gdk/docs/gdk-dev/get-started/overviews/set-up-dev-pc).
3. Defer dedicated kit, 10 GbE switch, 4K HDR display, and console-specific purchases until a console build and test plan justifies them. Microsoft recommends NVMe and wired networking for console deployment; its 10 GbE configuration is for faster supported dev-kit transfers.
4. Record quote date, seller of record, warranty, taxes, shipping, accessible peripherals, recurring software costs, and alternative configurations in the [quote comparison](../templates/quote-comparison.md). Explain each cost through the work tasks and access needs, not the brand.
