# Agency Review Boundary

The `participant-agency-review` branch is a review workspace for materials that a participant, counselor, procurement official, community-partnership specialist, benefits professional, accessibility reviewer, or other authorized reviewer may evaluate.

## What review means

Review may:

- identify missing information;
- request corrections;
- assess whether the curriculum relates to an employment goal;
- route Loptr Lab to the correct provider process;
- document an authorized agency determination.

Review does not by itself:

- create a contract;
- approve statewide vendor status;
- authorize services for every participant;
- guarantee payment;
- convert project work into employment;
- protect benefits;
- authorize disclosure of private participant information.

Only an agency official acting within documented authority can issue an agency approval. A participant can request, support, and document review, but cannot confer agency authority.

## GitHub privacy rule

Do not place referrals, authorizations, disability information, medical records, benefits records, housing information, financial records, releases, case numbers, government identifiers, or private correspondence in this branch.

Agency decisions should be recorded publicly only as a sanitized status statement when disclosure is authorized. The underlying decision belongs in the private compliance record.
