# Video track

## Work tasks

Plan and capture footage, ingest media, edit picture and sound, review detail, create captions, render, deliver, and archive projects.

## Required capabilities

- Storage capacity, sustained transfer speed, and backup appropriate to actual media formats
- Display and scaling that support timeline, text, focus, and color-dependent work
- Compute for the selected codecs, effects, resolution, and delivery schedule
- Accessible shortcuts, alternate input, transcription, and caption-review tools

## Plan

Test with a representative project rather than marketing claims. Document minimum delivery requirements separately from preferred turnaround time. Consider proxies, external storage, shared facilities, or remote rendering as alternatives.
