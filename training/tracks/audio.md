# Audio track

## Work tasks

Record, edit, arrange, mix, monitor, collaborate, export, document rights and versions, and archive sessions.

## Required capabilities

- Stable low-latency recording and playback for the actual track and plugin load
- Accessible controls, meters, labeling, navigation, and keyboard or alternate-input operation
- Suitable input/output interfaces and monitoring for the work environment
- Storage, naming, versioning, and backup that preserve creative continuity

## Plan

Separate recording, editing, mixing, live, and mobile requirements. Minimum specifications must be demonstrated against a representative session. Preferred items require a task or accessibility justification, not an assumption that more expensive equipment is automatically vocationally necessary.
