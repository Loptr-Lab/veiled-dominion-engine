Career tracks

Choose the work before the equipment

See development equipment requirements →

Choose the work you want to do before comparing equipment. For every track, document: work tasks, software and workflow requirements, compute requirements, accessibility considerations, minimum and preferred capabilities, and questions requiring individual assessment.

TrackBegin with these tasks
Software developmentEdit code, run tests, manage versions, collaborate.
Game developmentBuild and test interactive systems, assets, and levels.
VideoCapture, edit, review, caption, render, and deliver.
AudioRecord, edit, mix, monitor, export, and archive.
DesignResearch, compose, prototype, review, and export.
Hybrid creative technologyCombine code, media, interactive tools, and production workflows.

Optional project exercise: maker game systems compares a proposed digital pinball pilot with an unbuilt Duet LED chessboard design study. Their project repositories retain their own rules and approval boundaries.

After choosing a track, use the accessibility guidance, then build a technology plan.

Do not begin with a brand or device model. A product is justified only after the work and required capabilities are documented.
