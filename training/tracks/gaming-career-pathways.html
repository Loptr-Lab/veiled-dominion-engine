Explore the Gaming Industry Job Tracker

Open Gaming Industry Job Tracker ↗

Built by gaming career coach Chris Tran, the independent tracker collects open postings from studio applicant-tracking-system boards and refreshes its listings regularly. It includes role and studio browsing, location and remote filters, entry-level listings, salary information, and hiring reports. It links learners toward original studio postings rather than making Loptr Lab a job-board intermediary.

Before interpreting counts, read the tracker’s methodology: listings are collected from monitored studio applicant-tracking-system boards, and a listing disappearing does not by itself prove a hire or layoff. The tracker is independent; it is not affiliated with or endorsed by Loptr Lab.

A learner-controlled evidence checklist

  • What did I make, test, document, or improve?
  • What part did I personally contribute, and what came from collaborators or tools?
  • Can another person reproduce or review the result?
  • Are permissions, licenses, credits, and third-party assets documented?
  • Have I removed private, confidential, or identifying information?
  • Does the evidence match the actual requirements in the role posting?

This is a reflection aid, not an employability score. No automated ranking, application submission, disability disclosure, or purchase is required. Learners decide what to keep private and what to share.

Use the job market as research, not a promise

Compare several current postings for the same role family. Separate recurring requirements from one-off preferences, note experience level and location, and check the original studio listing before relying on a summary. Job availability changes quickly; a tracker count is a dated snapshot, not a forecast or guarantee of work.
