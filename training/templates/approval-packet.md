# Approval packet checklist

- [ ] Vocational goal and work tasks
- [ ] Functional barriers and essential capabilities
- [ ] Minimum, preferred, and alternative configurations
- [ ] Training plan, if relevant
- [ ] Current source-verification records
- [ ] Comparable dated quotes
- [ ] Questions requiring a human decision
- [ ] Written authorization requirements identified
- [ ] Private records excluded from public copies
- [ ] Final packet reviewed for unsupported eligibility or funding claims
