# Minnesota VRS Participant Path

_Status checked against official Minnesota DEED pages on September 21, 2026. Requirements can change; confirm them with VRS._

## Current status

Loptr Lab is not currently represented as a Minnesota VRS Community Partner, contracted provider, or guaranteed funding source. This repository may be presented to a VRS counselor as a proposed training option. It is not itself an authorization.

## Participant path

1. **Connect with Minnesota VRS.** Use the official [VRS information page](https://mn.gov/deed/job-seekers/disabilities/) or [interest form](https://www.mnworkforceone.com/Resources/InterestFormForAgencyAdd.aspx?a=100000170).
2. **Complete eligibility and employment planning.** Tell the counselor the employment goal, disability-related barriers, needed accommodations, and why this curriculum may be useful.
3. **Give the counselor the review packet.** Provide the repository link and the materials on the `participant-agency-review` branch when available.
4. **Ask for a written determination.** Ask whether the activity is necessary for the employment goal, what service category applies, who may provide it, what costs may be covered, and what records are required.
5. **Wait for written authorization.** Do not describe participation as VRS-funded, referred, or approved before receiving a written answer from VRS.
6. **Keep private records private.** Send benefit, medical, housing, financial, disability, or case documents only through the channel designated by VRS—not GitHub.
7. **Report changes.** Follow the counselor's instructions for attendance, progress, work activity, wages, self-employment, and changes in income.

## Questions to ask the counselor

- Can this training support the employment goal in my Individualized Plan for Employment?
- Is individual approval possible while Loptr Lab works toward provider readiness?
- Must an already-approved provider deliver or sponsor the service?
- What service category and authorization are required?
- What costs, dates, hours, accommodations, deliverables, and progress reports must be documented?
- Does the activity count as training, assessment, work experience, internship, employment, self-employment, volunteering, or something else?
- Must wages be paid for any project activity?
- What must be reported to VRS, Social Security, housing, Medicaid, or another program?
- Who can provide qualified benefits counseling before money or work activity begins?

## Decision record

Record the agency's answer as one of:

- `APPROVED IN WRITING`
- `APPROVED WITH CONDITIONS`
- `MORE INFORMATION REQUIRED`
- `DIFFERENT PROVIDER REQUIRED`
- `NOT APPROVED`
- `UNRESOLVED — DO NOT RELY ON`

An individual approval does not make Loptr Lab a statewide VRS vendor and does not approve services for another participant.
